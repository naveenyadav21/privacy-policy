Delete your Sudoku account and data

This page is for Sudoku 16x16 & 9x9 (also listed as Classic Sudoku Pro) on Google Play, by the developer Anime Gamers.

You only have an account if you signed in with Google. If you played as a guest, your games stay on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes them.

How to ask

  1. Email naveenyadav.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Sudoku account".
  2. Send it from the Google address you signed in with, so we can match it to your account.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in record: your name, email address and account ID.
  • Your saved games and statistics.
  • Any feedback you sent while signed in, including a reply address you gave.

What we keep

  • Analytics and crash reports are not linked to your name or email. Google stores them and removes them on its own schedule.
  • Advertising data is held by Google under its privacy policy. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • We keep limited records only where the law requires it.

Signing out or uninstalling does not delete your account. Please ask using the steps above.
